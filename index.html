SAMPLE 04 · ONLINE RESEARCHSupplier Comparison Report

SAMPLE PROJECT
Objective
Help a small online shop choose a packaging supplier based on price, minimum order, and speed.
Task performed
Gathered supplier details, compared them in one table, and wrote a short recommendation.
Tools used
Google Chrome, Google Sheets, Google Docs
Process
Searched for suppliers, recorded the same details for each one, computed price per piece and minimum order cost, and noted where each detail was found.

Final output

Request

The client sells handmade soaps online and needs kraft mailer boxes. Compare three suppliers and recommend one.

Sample data notice: the suppliers below are invented for demonstration. In real work, each row would include the source link and the date I checked it.

Comparison

SupplierLocationPrice per 100 pcs (₱)Price per piece (₱)Minimum orderCost of minimum order (₱)Lead timeContact
GreenWrap TradingManila1,85018.50100 pcs1,8505 dayssales@example.com
BoxNest PackagingCavite1,72017.20200 pcs3,4407 dayshello@example.com
PackRight SuppliesQuezon City2,05020.5050 pcs1,0253 daysorders@example.com

Findings

  • BoxNest Packaging has the lowest price per piece, but its 200-piece minimum means the highest upfront cost.
  • PackRight Supplies is the fastest and has the smallest minimum order, but costs the most per piece.
  • GreenWrap Trading sits in the middle on price, minimum order, and lead time.

Recommendation

For a first order, GreenWrap Trading offers the best balance. If monthly volume grows past 200 boxes, switching to BoxNest Packaging would lower the cost per piece.
